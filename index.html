01 🔥 Sizzling in Skillet
Sweet & Bursting • Somerset Soil

Somerset Organic Vine Cherry Tomatoes

400g local on-the-vine

Organic cherry tomatoes from local Somerset growers. When blistered in screaming-hot extra virgin olive oil, their skins burst into an instant sweet pan sauce.

Kino Kitchen Tip: Keep the green calyx caps attached! They release a fresh, peppery greenhouse perfume into the hot olive oil.
02 🔥 Sizzling in Skillet
Tangy & Creamy • 100% Vegan

Cultured Artisan Vegan Feta Blocks

200g almond-milk cultured block

Our kitchen’s favourite cultured plant-based feta with Greek oregano and lemon brine. Sizzled directly against the cast-iron base until deeply caramelized on one side.

Kino Kitchen Tip: Keep chunky golden nuggets that melt into velvety cream when emulsified with starchy pasta water.
03 🔥 Sizzling in Skillet
Fragrant & Herbal • Fresh Picked

Fresh Sweet Genovese Basil Leaves

1 generous bunch, hand-picked

Tender, aromatic broad basil leaves. Folded in right at the tossing climax so the residual skillet heat gently wilts them into glossy emerald ribbons.

Kino Kitchen Tip: Tear gently with warm fingertips rather than slicing with steel; crushing by hand prevents bruising the delicate aniseed essential oils.
04 🔥 Sizzling in Skillet
Nutty & Caramelized

Crispy Sautéed Garlic Slivers

4 fat cloves, paper-thin sliced

Translucent garlic slivers that dance in simmering extra virgin olive oil until pale straw-golden and crisp, infusing every droplet of fat with sweet allium depth.

Kino Kitchen Tip: Watch like a hawk: garlic travels from aromatic gold to acrid bitter charcoal in seconds. Always keep your skillet moving.
05 🔥 Sizzling in Skillet
Al Dente & Coarse • Bronze Die

Bronze-Die Ribbed Durum Rigatoni

350g artisan durum wheat

Coarse-surfaced durum wheat pasta tubes with wide ridges designed specifically to cup bubbling tomato juices and molten vegan feta inside the tube.

Kino Kitchen Tip: Boil 2 minutes shy of packet time in salted water; finish cooking directly in the sizzling skillet with a splash of starchy water.
06 🔥 Sizzling in Skillet
Fiery & Mineral • Cornish Salt

Calabrian Chili Flakes & Cornish Sea Salt

1 tsp flakes + mineral sea salt

Crushed dried pepper flakes giving warming citrus-tinged heat, paired with pyramid crystals of Cornish sea salt for crisp textural punch.

Kino Kitchen Tip: A pinch of fiery heat cuts right through the creamy richness of the cultured vegan feta, creating perfect culinary balance.

Cooking together is at the very heart of Cafe Kino. Operating as a non-profit workers' co-operative in Stokes Croft since 2010, our kitchen believes in delicious, vibrant, accessible plant-based food made from scratch with care.

This sizzling one-pan rigatoni celebrates the theatrical beauty of the cast-iron skillet. By searing sweet Somerset vine tomatoes directly alongside artisanal cultured vegan feta, you create a luscious, dairy-free sauce in minutes. That decisive flick of the skillet emulsifies the starches into creamy velvet without a single drop of dairy.

“Toss with confidence — that swift flick of the skillet emulsifies sweet tomato nectar, olive oil and starchy pasta water into luscious silk without a drop of dairy.”

— Cafe Kino Kitchen Collective, 108 Stokes Croft

Cooking in a single cast-iron skillet also honours our low-waste, energy-conscious ethos: maximum flavor and crispy edges with minimal washing up.

The Step-by-Step Cooking Method

1

Salt the water & drop the rigatoni

Bring a large pot of generously salted water to a rolling boil. Drop in the bronze-die rigatoni and boil for 9 minutes — 2 minutes shy of al dente. Reserve a mugful of that liquid gold (the starchy pasta water) before draining.

2

Warm the skillet with garlic & chili

Place your cast-iron skillet over medium heat. Pour in four tablespoons of good extra virgin olive oil. Add the sliced garlic and dried chili flakes. Swirl gently for 45 seconds until the garlic turns pale straw-golden and fills the kitchen with nutty aroma.

3

Blister the tomatoes & sear the vegan feta

Tumble in the whole vine cherry tomatoes and rustic chunks of cultured vegan feta. Let them sit undisturbed for 2 minutes to get that glorious golden crust on the base. Gently squash half the tomatoes with a wooden spoon so they burst in fragrant steam.

4

The Great Skillet Toss

Tip the drained, steaming rigatoni directly into the skillet with a generous splash of reserved pasta water. Now comes the flick: lift the skillet, tilt forward, and flick upward with confidence to toss the ingredients through the sauce until every ridge is cloaked in emulsion.

5

Scatter fresh basil & gather round

Take the skillet off the heat. Scatter over the hand-torn sweet basil leaves, a final drizzle of raw peppery olive oil, and flaky Cornish sea salt. Serve straight from the sizzling skillet in the middle of the table with crusty local sourdough.
